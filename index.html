SINCE 1978

A legacy built over generations.

In 1978, in the bustling lanes near Station Road, Pilibhit, our family started with an uncompromising principle: never cut corners on milk, curd, or ghee. While others hurried their preparation, we slow-boiled fresh buffalo milk in heavy iron and brass kadhais, and churned dahi the age-old way using wooden bilonas.

The result was a lassi so remarkably thick and crowned with rich clotted rabdi that visitors from Bareilly and Lucknow playfully proclaimed it "Badnaam" (infamous) — because once you indulge in a single kulhad, your hunger for any other meal that day is completely ruined!

Wooden Bilona Churning

Retains the natural buttery foam and sweet live probiotics of whole milk dahi.

Terracotta Kulhads

Natural earthen cups that chill the lassi while infusing earthy mineral fragrance.

100% Asli Desi Ghee

Every jalebi, samosa, and ladoo is fried purely in certified cow and buffalo ghee.

Daily Slow Khoya

Caramelized reduced milk without starch or fillers for our iconic Pilibhit Doodh Peda.
