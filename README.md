# Badnaam Lassi & Sweets House 🥛🍬

> **A clean, modern, and fully responsive website for Badnaam Lassi & Sweets House, the iconic sweet shop in Pilibhit, Uttar Pradesh.**

🔗 **Live Website**: [https://kushinprogress.github.io/sweet-shop-site/](https://kushinprogress.github.io/sweet-shop-site/)

---

## 🌟 Overview

**Badnaam Lassi & Sweets House** (बदनाम लस्सी एवं मिष्ठान भंडार) is legendary for its slow-churned, thick **Kulhad Rabdi Lassi**, caramelized **Pilibhit Doodh Peda**, hot **Desi Ghee Jalebi**, crispy samosas, and traditional sweets made from 100% pure milk and desi ghee.

This website brings the warmth and luxury of Indian culinary heritage online with a royal cream, brown, maroon, and gold aesthetic, smooth scroll interactions, an interactive order tray, and full mobile optimization.

---

## 🎨 Design System & Aesthetic

- **Palette**: Warm Cream (`#FAF6F0`), Deep Royal Maroon (`#781324`), Earthen Clay Brown (`#2C1710`), Regal Gold (`#D4AF37`), and Pure Veg Green (`#1E7E34`).
- **Typography**: 
  - Headings: `Marcellus` & `Playfair Display` (Serif elegance)
  - Body & UI: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Clean, modern legibility)
- **Motifs**: Custom SVG royal crest, Indian mandala watermarks, and floral dividers.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Hero Showcase**:
   - Signature Rabdi Lassi presented in an earthen terracotta kulhad with thick malai, pistachios, almonds, and saffron.
   - Heritage trust badges and instant action buttons (*Explore Menu*, *Order on WhatsApp*, *Find Location*).

2. **Heritage & Story (About Section)**:
   - The folklore behind the playful moniker *"Badnaam"* (*"स्वाद ऐसा जो बदनाम कर दे"*).
   - 4 Pillars: Wooden Bilona Churning, Pure Khoya Craft, 100% Desi Ghee, and Terracotta Kulhad cooling.
   - Interactive milestone counters (45+ Years, 1,200+ Litres Churned Daily, 35+ Sweets).

3. **Categorized Menu & Live Search**:
   - Filter tabs: *All Delicacies*, *Signature Lassi*, *Heritage Sweets*, *Desi Ghee Snacks*, *Festive Gift Hampers*.
   - Live real-time search filtering by dish names, Hindi text, ingredients, or tags.
   - Realistic food photography, veg certification badges, prices in ₹ (INR), and description of ingredients.

4. **Interactive WhatsApp Order Tray**:
   - Add delicacies to a slide-out order basket with quantity controls (`+` / `-`).
   - Dynamic badge notifications on navigation and mobile bars.
   - Automatic subtotal calculation.
   - **One-Click WhatsApp Dispatch**: Generates a pre-formatted, polite order list ready to send directly to the shop’s phone number.

5. **Customer Reviews**:
   - 4.9★ rating based on 1,480+ Google Reviews.
   - Testimonials from local foodies and highway travelers visiting from Bareilly, Lucknow, and Delhi.

6. **Photo Gallery & Lightbox**:
   - Curated photo moments of lassi, golden jalebis, doodh peda, gulab jamuns, samosas, and shop kitchen.
   - Responsive masonry layout with interactive full-screen Lightbox modal (keyboard navigation & zoom support).

7. **Visit & Contact Section**:
   - Complete address: Station Road, Near Gandhi Stadium Chowk, Pilibhit, Uttar Pradesh 262001.
   - Operating Hours: **7:00 AM – 11:00 PM** (All 7 Days).
   - Embedded responsive Google Map.
   - Direct Call and Navigation buttons.
   - Wedding & Festival Bulk Catering inquiry form.

8. **Mobile First & Accessibility**:
   - Mobile bottom quick action bar (*Call*, *WhatsApp*, *Directions*, *Order*).
   - Smooth scrolling, sticky transparent-to-solid navigation bar.
   - Schema.org JSON-LD LocalBusiness & Restaurant metadata for search engine indexing.

## 💻 Local Development

Run any standard local HTTP server:

```bash

python3 -m http.server 8080

# Node (npx)
npx serve
```

Visit `http://localhost:8080/` in your browser.
